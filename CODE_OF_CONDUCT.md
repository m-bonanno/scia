# Code of conduct

Scia follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1, in every place of the project: its issues, its pull requests, its reviews and anywhere someone speaks for it.

In short: be kind and patient, assume good faith, and keep to the problem, not the person. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing someone's private details are not accepted, from anyone.

## Reporting

Report what you saw privately, through this repository's [private reporting](https://github.com/m-bonanno/scia/security/advisories/new) (Security → Report a vulnerability), saying it is about conduct. No address is published: that form reaches the owner alone, and nobody else reads it.

The owner reads every report, answers it, and decides what follows, as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines say: from a word in private to a ban from the project. Whoever reports is not named without their consent.
