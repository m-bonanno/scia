# Scia — design

Captures of the real app: its page, read over the DevTools protocol while the app runs on a scratch profile and downloads from local test hosts. The page only — the window's own buttons, which the system draws at the right of the title bar, are not in them, except in [`window/`](./window/), which is the whole window as the system composes it. The app's icon, at the end, is the exception: its captures say how each was made.

The app wears one of nine themes, each in a light and a dark variant that follows the system: [`themes.md`](./themes.md) says where every colour of each comes from. The captures below are in the default one, Black Tie.

The captures of Settings were taken from the built app, started by Electron on a scratch profile as the E2E suite starts it — by CI's E2E job, those named below — which offers "Beta versions" as a Windows install does, and never updates itself. The folder in the captures of Settings belongs to that scratch profile, deleted after the capture. The AllDebrid account in them is the simulated service's (`apps/server/src/testing/alldebrid-server.ts`): no such account exists, and the key typed for these captures was made up. So is the code of the two captures of a code: the simulated service gave it, and AllDebrid knows nothing of it. The magnet of the four captures of a magnet is made up too: no torrent has its hash, and its files, in the two taken while AllDebrid fetched it, were local test files. So are the magnets of the four captures of the panel, which the simulated service listed as added on its site, and the links beside them, served by local test hosts and the simulated service.

The light variant of a capture is the page told that the system asks for light (the protocol's emulated `prefers-color-scheme`), on a system that was dark: the page's colours are the light variant's, and the window's own buttons, which are not in a capture, were not seen in it. The captures retaken in #155 — the eight of Settings and [`window/`](./window/) — set Electron's own variant instead (`nativeTheme.themeSource`), which moves the page and main's colours together as a light system would; the system itself was dark, and a Windows set to light has not been seen (#58).

The three captures of a notice over Settings were taken by CI's E2E job (`apps/desktop/e2e/notices.e2e.test.ts`, "takes the captures of a notice over Settings"), and so were, for "Change…" (#276) and "Support Scia" (#271), `settings.png`, `two-ways-to-connect.png`, `connect-a-service.png`, `connect-with-a-code.png` and their light twins where they have one (`apps/desktop/e2e/settings.e2e.test.ts`, "takes the captures of Settings", against the simulated service), and `downloads-paused.png` (`apps/desktop/e2e/paused.e2e.test.ts`, "takes the capture of a paused download beside the running ones", its downloads from hosts of the runner that send slowly, and a failure answered with 404), from the built app on a scratch profile of the runner, the page given its size over the protocol; they were read before they were committed. The folder they show is the runner's, deleted after the run, and the version beside the name is that of a build that is not a release, `0.0.0`.

Since #150 the field says "Paste links or magnets" while AllDebrid is connected. The ten captures that show the field with AllDebrid connected were taken again for it by CI's E2E job (its run on #287's head f778f8d, with develop's #272 merged), from the built app on a scratch profile of the runner, against the simulated service and local hosts, and read before they were committed: `a-magnet.png` and its light twin, `via-a-service.png`, `a-failure-in-full.png` and the four of the account's panel (`apps/desktop/e2e/magnets.e2e.test.ts`, "takes the captures"), and `settings.png` and its light twin (`settings.e2e.test.ts`, above, which connects AllDebrid before it opens Settings again). They show the version beside the name and, but for Settings, the button of the account's magnets in the title bar; the narrow panel's running download is a plain file, no longer a page's video.

From #88 the logo in the title bar and in the empty list draws the mark as the app's icon does. From #133 the title bar names the version beside "Scia" (or offers an update there); the captures above taken before it show the title bar without it, and are retaken in #156.

`update-while-downloading.png` and its light twin show the note's words before #159 and #257 ("Scia is downloading. … installs when you quit Scia."); it now says "<version> installs when you quit Scia. Pause your downloads to restart now." The E2E suite cannot offer an update, so both are retaken, with the note's first look in a packaged build, in the owner's sitting on the release candidate of `v1.0.0` (the owner, 23 November 2026), carried by #156.

A capture is retaken in the PR that changes what it shows (`apps/renderer/CLAUDE.md`, "Behaviour"; the product designer checks it).

[`social-preview.png`](./social-preview.png) is no capture of the app: it is the image GitHub shows when the repository is linked (Settings → Social preview), 1280 by 640, in Black Tie's dark variant with the app's mark. Chromium draws it from [`social-preview.html`](./social-preview.html), which says how, and it is uploaded by hand: GitHub has no API for it (#310). It is drawn again, and uploaded again, when the name, the tagline, the mark or Black Tie's dark colours change.
