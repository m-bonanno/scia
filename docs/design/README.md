# Scia — design

Captures of the real app: its page, read over the DevTools protocol while the app runs on a scratch profile and downloads from local test hosts. The page only — the window's own buttons, which the system draws at the right of the title bar, are not in them. The app's icon, at the end, is the exception: its captures say how each was made.

The app wears one of nine themes, each in a light and a dark variant that follows the system: [`themes.md`](./themes.md) says where every colour of each comes from. The captures below are in the default one, Black Tie.

The two captures of the whole dialog were taken from the built app, started by Electron on a scratch profile as the E2E suite starts it, which offers "Beta versions" as a Windows install does, and never updates itself. The pairing code and the folder in the three captures of Settings belong to that scratch profile, deleted after the capture: the code opens nothing. The AllDebrid account in them is the simulated service's (`apps/server/src/testing/alldebrid-server.ts`): no such account exists, and the key typed for these captures was made up. So is the code of the two captures of a code: the simulated service gave it, and AllDebrid knows nothing of it. The magnet of the four captures of a magnet is made up too: no torrent has its hash, and its files, in the two taken while AllDebrid fetched it, were local test files.

The light variant of a capture is the page told that the system asks for light (the protocol's emulated `prefers-color-scheme`), on a system that was dark: the page's colours are the light variant's, and the window's own buttons, which are not in a capture, were not seen in it.

From #133 the title bar names the version beside "Scia" (or offers an update there); the captures above taken before it show the title bar without it, and are retaken in #156.

A capture is retaken in the PR that changes what it shows (`apps/renderer/CLAUDE.md`, "Behaviour"; the product designer checks it).

Not captured yet: Settings with a folder refused, the key's form in a light variant, a connected service that says its key is no longer accepted, the open list of a menu of Settings — the speed limit's, the theme's — (the system draws it outside the page, so no capture of the page shows it; it was read from the system in the review of #52 — the theme's menu in every theme and both variants, the speed limit's once: its rows are the theme's `tonal` and `foreground`, its chosen row Chromium's own blue — #58), the title bar with a limit in the light variant, Settings and "Run" in a theme that is not the default one, the empty list, several notices stacked, a notice over Settings at the window's default height of 720 px (where it lies over the bottom of the dialog), the extension's popup and options, and the notifications the system draws for Scia (the one of the first time it hides, the one of a download that ends, and the one of links taken from the clipboard): no capture of the page shows them, and nobody has looked at the first two — the third the owner saw and clicked in the hand-run of #131.

## The app's icon

The mark on a tile, in the default theme's dark variant (`#e3e3e3` on `#111111`; the owner, #81). Its one source is [`apps/desktop/assets/icon.svg`](../../apps/desktop/assets/icon.svg); every icon of the app is drawn from it by `npm run icons -w @scia/desktop` and committed (`apps/desktop/CLAUDE.md`).
