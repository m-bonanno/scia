# Scia — integrations

Every site, service and program Scia reaches beyond the host of a link it was given, and what each one adds. One row per integration. **A PR that adds one, or changes what one does, changes its row** (`CLAUDE.md`, "Documentation discipline"). What was seen of each, and how: [evidence.md](./evidence.md). The order of the work: [roadmap.md](./roadmap.md).

Every integration keeps to "Respect the hosts" (`CLAUDE.md`). Scia fetches only what the person can already reach. It never circumvents DRM, and never evades a paywall, a login the person does not have, or a geographic restriction.

## Services the person connects

## Readers of pages

A link whose host answers a web page is read for what it plays or lists. The reader runs on this computer and fetches nothing for the person: the engine downloads what it found, as any file.

| yt-dlp (shipped)            | The video a page plays, on the sites yt-dlp has a reader of its own for (never its generic one). Scia takes the best it can fetch: one file; an HLS stream made into an mp4; or a picture and its sound offered apart, joined into one mp4. No ffmpeg. yt-dlp is kept up to date by Scia from its signed releases. | [0009](../adr/0009-the-video-of-a-page.md), [0010](../adr/0010-a-stream-made-into-an-mp4-inside-scia.md), [0011](../adr/0011-yt-dlp-kept-up-to-date.md), [0021](../adr/0021-picture-and-sound-joined-into-one-mp4.md) | #89, #97, #98, #99, #100 | One file seen in the real app with archive.org.
